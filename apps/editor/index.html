Click an empty cell to add a note and drag to lengthen it; drag a note to move it or its right end to resize it. Keys: arrows move the cursor, Space or Enter adds or selects, Alt+arrows move and Shift+Left/Right resize the selected note, Delete removes it.

A single-note sound plays one tone: set its waveform, frequency, envelope and duration on the left, and optionally slide it to another frequency, add vibrato or fade it out. Choose Notes as its type to compose a short jingle of up to 64 notes on a piano roll.
